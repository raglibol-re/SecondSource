How a check works

One job takes about twenty seconds and costs about 13 cents of test money in evidence.

  1. Hire

    An agent, or a person in Sokosumi, sends the headline. Payment waits in Masumi escrow on Cardano.

  2. Buy evidence

    SecondSource pays per call over x402 on AirnodeHub: posts from the accounts that matter, the Benzinga newswire, and the market tape.

  3. Check signatures

    Every answer is signed by the Airnode that served it. Anything with the wrong signer, request or age is thrown out before use.

  4. Judge

    A language model reads only that evidence. Its answer comes back through an Airnode too, so the judgment is signed.

  5. Deliver

    The verdict, the reasoning, the cited evidence and every receipt. The result hash is logged on Cardano.

Every answer carries a signature

A screenshot of a post can be faked and a deleted post leaves nothing behind. A signed response says who served it, what was asked and when.

Loading a real signed response…
airnode
The address that signed. We compare it to the address published in that API's own spec.
requestHash
A hash of the exact question. We recompute it from what we asked, so an answer cannot be moved to another question.
timestamp
When the Airnode signed. Stale answers are rejected.
data
The provider's answer, byte for byte. Change one character and the signature no longer matches.
signature
EIP-191 over all of the above. Anyone can check it, including a smart contract.

Who hires it

Trading agents

Call SecondSource before any order that a headline triggered. Only act on CONFIRMED, and keep the receipts for the audit trail.

Trading desks

Put a check between the news feed and execution. A vault can refuse to trade unless a signed CONFIRMED verdict is attached.

Newsrooms

Check what a quoted account really posted, and keep proof of it even after the post is edited or deleted.

Built on two networks

Cardano, Masumi and Sokosumi

SecondSource is a Coworker that gets paid. Buyers hire it through Masumi escrow on Preprod, and the decision hash goes on chain.

API3 AirnodeHub

Every input is bought per call over x402 in aUSD on Base Sepolia, from Airnodes that sign each response: TwitterAPI.io, Benzinga, TickerLayer, Nodary and OpenRouter.
